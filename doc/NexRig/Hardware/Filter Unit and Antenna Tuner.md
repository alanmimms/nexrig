# Filter Unit & Antenna Tuner

Impedance Domain: 200Ω Single-Ended

Power Handling: 100W Continuous / 150W Peak

Filter Topology: 5-Bank Switched LPF Array

Tuner Topology: Switched L-Network (Binary)

## 1. Overview

The Filter Unit operates entirely in the **200Ω impedance domain**. Operating at 200Ω instead of 50Ω reduces the RF current by 50% (0.7A RMS vs 1.4A RMS at 100W), allowing for higher-Q inductors and reduced thermal stress on capacitor banks.

The unit performs two critical functions:

1. **Harmonic Suppression:** A bank of 5 low-pass filters removes odd-order harmonics generated by the PA switching (even harmonics are canceled by the H-Bridge topology).
    
2. **Impedance Matching:** An automatic antenna tuner matches the antenna impedance to the 200Ω system impedance before the final transformation to 50Ω.
    

## 2. 5-Bank LPF Array

The Low-Pass Filter array consolidates the amateur bands into 5 shared filter banks to optimize board space and cost.

### 2.1 Filter Bands

|**Bank**|**Cutoff Freq (fc​)**|**Coverage**|**Order**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Low**|~2.5 MHz|160m|7th Order Chebyshev|
|**Mid-1**|~6.0 MHz|80m, 60m|7th Order Chebyshev|
|**Mid-2**|~11.0 MHz|40m, 30m|7th Order Chebyshev|
|**High-1**|~22.0 MHz|20m, 17m, 15m|7th Order Chebyshev|
|**High-2**|~32.0 MHz|12m, 10m|7th Order Chebyshev|

### 2.2 High-Power Magnetics

To prevent core saturation at 100W (especially on 160m), the LPF inductors must use physically larger cores than previous 50W designs.

- **160m / 80m:** Hand-wound **T80-2** or **T106-2** (Red Iron Powder) toroids.
    
- **High Bands:** **T80-6** (Yellow) or high-Q air-core inductors for 10m.
    
- **Thermal:** Inductors are spaced to prevent mutual coupling and allow airflow.
    

### 2.3 Switching Logic

- **Relays:** **Omron G2RL-2** (DPDT, 8A).
    
    - **Selection:** Standard signal relays (2A) are insufficient for 100W LPF switching due to potential mismatch currents. The G2RL-2 provides an 8A safety margin.
        
    - **Configuration:** Relays switch both Input and Output of the selected filter. Unused filters are grounded to improve isolation.
        

## 3. Antenna Tuner

The tuner uses a binary-switched L-Network that can be configured as High-Pass or Low-Pass (C-series/L-shunt or L-series/C-shunt) to match a wide range of complex impedances.

### 3.1 Tuning Strategy

Unlike traditional "hunt-and-peck" tuners, the NexRig uses **Deterministic Tuning**:

1. The Receiver's Bruene Coupler measures the complex impedance vector ($R + jX$) in the 200Ω domain.
    
2. The host CPU calculates the exact L and C values required to conjugate match this impedance to 200Ω.
    
3. The relays are "cold switched" (RF power off) to the calculated state.
    
4. Tuning completes in < 50ms.
    

### 3.2 Components

- **Inductor Bank:** Binary sequence (0.25µH, 0.5µH, 1µH... 16µH).
    
    - **Cores:** **T130-2** or stacked **T106-2** toroids are required for the high-inductance stages to handle 100W without saturation or excessive heating.
        
- **Capacitor Bank:** Binary sequence (10pF, 20pF... 1000pF).
    
    - **Voltage Rating:** **≥500V**. In the 200Ω domain, resonant voltages can peak significantly higher than in 50Ω systems.
        
    - **Type:** C0G/NP0 Ceramic or Silver Mica for high Q and stability.
        
- **Switching:** **Omron G2RL-2** (8A).
    
    - **Critical:** High-SWR loads (low impedance) can generate currents >3A. The 8A relays are mandatory here to prevent contact welding.
        

## 4. Impedance Transformation

### 4.1 Output Transformer (`T502`)

- **Function:** Transforms the corrected 200Ω signal down to the standard 50Ω antenna port.
    
- **Core:** Stacked **FT140-43** ferrite toroids.
    
- **Winding:** Bifilar winding with a **2:1 Turns Ratio** (4:1 Impedance Ratio).
    
    - 200Ω Primary : 50Ω Secondary.
